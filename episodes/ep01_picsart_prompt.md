# 1화 픽스아트(Picsart)용 프롬프트

`episodes/ep01_script_3min.md`의 1화 줄거리를 한 개의 영어 프롬프트로 묶은 것.
캐릭터 외형은 `characters.md`를 그대로 따른다. 가능하면 `assets/characters/`의 기준 이미지 4장을 레퍼런스로 함께 올린다.

## 전체 프롬프트

```
A heartwarming 2D children's cartoon about making first friends, set in Rainbow Valley, a small cozy island with a gentle sleeping volcano, green hills, a sparkling lake, a round forest and a rainbow sky.

Characters (keep exactly the same in every shot):
- Chiqui: a cute baby triceratops, soft lime-green body, cream belly, big sparkling dark eyes, rosy cheeks, three short rounded pale-peach horns, small rounded frill with tiny pastel dots, short stubby legs.
- Bronti: a gentle young brontosaurus, soft sky-blue body, pale cream belly, very long neck, calm kind half-closed eyes, lighter blue spots on the back.
- Pico: a playful baby pteranodon, butter-yellow body, light orange wings, small pointed crest, short rounded beak, cheeky grin.
- Tino: a shy baby tyrannosaurus, tangerine-orange body, cream belly, tiny arms held together shyly, gentle nervous smile, blushing cheeks, cute and not scary.

Story, in order:
1. Morning in Rainbow Valley. Chiqui arrives with a leaf bundle and looks around in wonder at the new home.
2. Chiqui hears laughter and peeks from behind a bush: Bronti, Pico and Tino are skipping stones at the lake.
3. Chiqui is too shy to say hello and sits alone under a tree.
4. Suddenly Chiqui's three horns glow one by one: pink, sky blue and golden yellow, with soft sparkles. Chiqui smiles bravely.
5. Chiqui waves at tall Bronti, who lowers her long neck kindly and smiles back. The pink horn glows.
6. Pico swoops down from the sky in a playful loop and lands in front of Chiqui, flapping happily. The blue horn glows.
7. Tino peeks out from behind a tree, then steps out and gives a small shy wave. The yellow horn glows.
8. The four friends play hide-and-seek in the meadow; Chiqui's three horns poke out from behind a rock and everyone laughs.
9. At sunset the four friends stand together on a grassy hill, bouncing happily and waving goodbye to the viewer under a rainbow.

Style: clean 2D children's cartoon, smooth soft shading, thick friendly outlines, simple rounded shapes, pastel colors, preschool animation look, warm and gentle mood, smooth cheerful motion, 16:9, no text, no subtitles.
```

## 사용 메모

- AI 영상 도구는 보통 한 번에 5~10초 영상을 만든다. 이 프롬프트로는 3분 전체가 아니라 1화의 요약 영상(티저·예고편)이 나온다고 보면 된다.
- 장면별로 따로 만들려면 위 `Characters`와 `Style` 문단은 그대로 두고, `Story`에서 해당 번호의 한 줄만 남겨 쓴다.
- 이미지 생성에 쓸 때는 `smooth cheerful motion`을 지우고 장면 한 줄만 남긴다.
- 숏폼(릴스·틱톡·쇼츠)용은 `16:9`를 `9:16 vertical`로 바꾼다.
